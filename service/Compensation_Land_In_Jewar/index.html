What is Compensation Land?

Understanding Compensation Land in the YEIDA Region

When development authorities acquire agricultural land for planned infrastructure and development projects, eligible landowners may receive compensation along with development-related benefits.

One such mechanism involves the allotment of an alternative compensation plot within a planned development area.

    • 01 — Land Acquisition: A development authority acquires land for infrastructure or planned development.
      02 — Compensation to Landowner: The original landowner receives the applicable compensation and development/release benefits.
      03 — Compensation Plot Allotment: Subject to applicable authority rules and conditions, an alternate development plot may be allotted to the landowner.
      04 — Plot Enters the Market: Eligible compensation plots may subsequently become available for transaction, creating an opportunity for investors to acquire land in strategically planned growth zones.

    • The exact entitlement, allotment, location, size, transferability and timelines are subject to applicable authority policies and individual documentation.

    Our Compensation Land Due-Diligence Process

    From Land Identification to Mutation

    Buying land in a regulated development corridor requires more than identifying a location and negotiating a price. At Noble Acre, we follow a structured due-diligence framework.

    • 01. Land Identification — dentify compensation-land opportunities based on location, investor objectives and availability.

    • 02. Khasra Verification — Verify relevant Khasra details and land boundaries against available revenue records.

    • 03. Khatauni Matching — Cross-check ownership details against relevant revenue documentation.

    • 04. Tehsil Verification — Undertake local-level verification relating to title and potential encumbrances.

    • 05. Title Validation — Review ownership history, succession documentation and other relevant title considerations.

    • 06. Registration — Support the investor through the formal registration process.

    • 07. Mutation — Assist with updating relevant land records to reflect the new ownership.
